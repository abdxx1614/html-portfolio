About Me

Hey, I’m Abdul Salaam — a tech enthusiast who likes building things, breaking things, and then figuring out why they broke in the first place. I come from an Electronics & Communication background, but my curiosity has taken me way beyond circuits and hardware—from AI and machine learning to data analytics, cybersecurity, and everything in between. I’m usually learning something new, experimenting with ideas, or having way too many tabs open at once. Basically, if there’s something interesting to figure out, I’m probably already curious about it.

When I’m not behind a screen, you’ll probably find me on a court. Tennis and pickleball are a huge part of my life. I love the competition, the adrenaline, the constant improvement, and that feeling when something you’ve been practicing finally clicks. I’m definitely the “one more game” kind of person—which somehow turns into three hours. Sports keep me competitive, disciplined, and grounded, while also reminding me that losing is just part of getting better.

At the end of the day, I’m a mix of tech, sports, curiosity, creativity, and a little bit of chaos. I like meeting new people, trying things I’ve never done before, taking on challenges, and occasionally falling down completely unnecessary 2 AM internet rabbit holes. I don’t have every step of the future planned—and I’m honestly cool with that. I’m here to keep learning, keep building, keep competing, and see where it takes me.
